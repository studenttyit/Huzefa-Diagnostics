Fever

Fever is a condition when the body temperature is higher than the normal temperature. It is accompanied by weakness, cold, shivering, loss of appetite, headache, and body ache. In most cases, the usual cause of fever is an infection. A mild fever is usually not a cause for concern and goes away by taking over-the-counter medications. But if it persists for a longer time, it can be alarming. High fever can be a sign of a serious infection or disease.

The best way to assess your fever is to measure your temperature. If you observe a rise in body temperature, book a hassle-free Fever Test At Home. A fever test can help diagnose serious diseases like typhoid, dengue, malaria, COVID-19, or bacterial infection. When is a Fever Test Prescribed?

A fever test is prescribed if the body temperature is persistently high along with other symptoms like chills and shivers or other signs of infection. Your doctor may also prescribe a fever test if:

  • An infant has a mild or high fever.
  • A child, less than 3 years of age, has a fever for more than 4 days.
  • A person with a medical history of serious diseases like cancer.
  • Fever that is accompanied by dizziness, vomiting, or diarrhoea.?

One should get a fever test done if they experience the following symptoms for more than a week:

  • Shivering
  • Dehydration
  • Fatigue
  • Muscle pain
  • Headache
  • Cold sweats

If you are experiencing serious symptoms like chest pain, breathlessness, or extreme chills, book a fever test online as soon as possible.
