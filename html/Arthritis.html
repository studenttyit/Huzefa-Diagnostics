Arthritis

Arthritis is a condition that affects your joints, causing inflammation or degeneration in them. It can affect joints in your feet, hips, knee, lower back, or hands. While there are many types of arthritis, the most common form is osteoarthritis, which occurs when the cartilage between your joints breaks down.

If you notice sudden stiffness or swelling in your joints, you might think it is a sprain or injury. However, a continuing pain or stiffness that has been there for a long period might be a sign of arthritis.

If left untreated, arthritis can cause severe injury or deformation of the joints and cause motor function issues in patients. Hence, getting an arthritis test early and getting diagnosed for the same is important

When is an Arthritis Test Prescribed?

The doctor may prescribe arthritis tests for you under the following circumstances:

  • If you have been suffering from unbearable, lasting pain for a long time
  • If you feel stiffness, pain, or swelling in your joints
  • If your range of movements has decreased significantly
  • If there is redness or inflammation over your joints

Furthermore, a doctor may recommend arthritis tests if you have a family history of the disease or any other related diseases. Such predisposition might lead to the development of arthritis in your body.

Why are Arthritis Tests Important?

Arthritis is a chronic condition that can last long and worsen over time.? It is important to get your arthritis condition confirmed through proper lab tests, as you can begin your treatment only after proper testing. There is no cure for arthritis, but there are treatments that can help ease the symptoms.

It is important to book an arthritis test and see your doctor for treatment to relieve your symptoms and prevent further joint damage. Further, you may have to undergo arthritis tests regularly to track your condition's development over time and distinguish between different types of arthritis.

How is An Arthritis Test Performed?

There are several types of arthritis, each requiring a different diagnostic test. However to narrow down the causes, your doctor may recommend various blood tests, the results of which may suggest the condition's existence. Some of these tests may include:

  • Erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR)
  • Anti-CCP antibodies test
  • Rheumatoid factor test
  • C-reactive protein (CRP) test
  • Antinuclear antibody (ANA) test

Some other tests that can diagnose the condition include MRIs, X-Rays, joint scans, and physical assessment tests.

If you are opting for blood tests at home, a medical practitioner will visit your home to collect your blood samples. They will take the collected samples to the lab for testing. In case of imaging tests and physical assessments, you will need to visit the lab.

Time Required For An Alcohol Test Report And What To Do Next

The usual time required for an alcohol test report is 24-48 hours. It also depends on the type of alcohol test that one has taken. A breathalyser test, however, shows immediate results.?

If alcohol levels in the blood are too high, the person is suggested to undergo rehabilitation therapy or kept on prescriptions that help to get rid of the patient's alcohol addiction. This is often done through a series of counselling sessions as well as medical prescriptions that help to control cravings and curb the physical side effects of addiction.
