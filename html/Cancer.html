Cancer

Diagnosing cancer in its earliest stages typically offers the greatest chance of a cure. Various studies indicate that screening tests can save lives by detecting cancer early for a few types of cancer. However, for some malignancies, screening tests are only advised for individuals at greater risk.

Some companies provide at-home cancer test kits, which include everything a patient needs to take a sample and send it in for analysis. These screening tests may require the patient to provide blood, stool, urine, saliva, or vaginal swab samples. Different test kits have different requirements.

These samples are then analyzed for various biomarkers (like antibodies, antigens or mutated genes) that can be used as an early warning system for the development of cancer. Some of these test kits may be recommended by doctors for those with a moderate risk of acquiring certain malignancies.

Every person shouldn't take every test. However, those having a higher chance of developing cancer may not benefit from undergoing a particular battery of diagnostic tests.

It is always better to consult your physician, who can help you weigh the pros and cons of using a home cancer screening kit. They will be able to help you decide which kits are best suited for you, keeping in mind your past medical history.

What's The Importance of a Cancer Test?

Cancer tests are of paramount importance, whether conducted at home or in a lab, because the best chance of beating cancer is to catch it early and get rid of it before the malignant cells spread to other parts of the body.

Time is of the essence, and the sooner you can detect it, the less fatal it becomes. Cancer can be diagnosed long before any symptoms appear.

Check out some of the important reasons why you should consider cancer tests:

  • Going for cancer tests helps diagnose it before it spreads throughout the body, thus making things easier and less worrisome for you. It also makes it easier to treat it without any fatalities.
  • The sooner you can detect your cancer, the better the odds are in your favour. You inadvertently increase your chances of beating it.
  • Early detection reduces the cost you have to incur for various treatments, tests, and chemotherapy sessions.
  • When you can detect cancer at an early stage, you can avoid experiencing any unpleasant physical manifestations that accompany the disease.
How Is Cancer Testing Performed?

When it comes to cancer tests, it is well-known that the odds of successfully treating it are considerably increased when the disease is detected early. You also have the added benefit of getting rid of the spread of malignant cells to other parts of the body.

Several diagnostic procedures have been developed to detect cancer early on. The following are some of them:

Biopsy

A biopsy is a procedure in which your doctor collects a sample of tissue or organ cells for further examination under a microscope to check for unusual-looking cells in the body. While there are various techniques for obtaining samples, the type and location of your cancer will help decide which biopsy method is best. A biopsy is considered the only test that is 100% sure to detect cancer cells in the body.

Physical Examination

Your doctor may perform a physical exam to examine if there are any unusual lumps present. Changes in skin tone or the size of an organ are also other preliminary signs of anomalies a doctor may look for during a physical exam to determine whether or not cancer is present.

Imaging Tests

You can avoid any invasive procedures by having your doctor use imaging scans to look at your bones and internal organs. CT scans, MRIs, PET scans, bone scans, ultrasounds, and X-rays are only some of the imaging tests that are commonly used to detect cancer.

Various Lab Tests

Your doctor may be able to detect cancer using the results of various lab tests, such as blood, urine, or even stool tests. Take, for instance, someone who may be suffering from leukemia. A routine blood test called a complete blood count, for instance, may help detect an abnormal amount or type of white blood cells in a person and better help the doctor diagnose cancer.

How Long Does It Take For The Result To Arrive?

It depends on which cancer test you opt for. Different tests have different timelines. You can get the results in either a few hours or a few days. It's best to consult with your doctor before going ahead with any cancer test.
