Vitamins

Vitamins are micronutrients that offer support for several crucial functions throughout the body. They are needed for normal cell functioning, growth, and development, further improving the immune system. There are 13 recognizable vitamins such as A, C, D, E, K, B1, B2, B3, B6, B12, biotin, folate, and pantothenic acid. From resisting infections and keeping nerves healthy to helping the blood clot quickly, these vitamins perform several crucial roles in the body. However, it is common for vitamin deficiencies to arise and go unnoticed. And this is why it is essential to take Vitamins Tests At Home.

Because vitamins play a vital role in the body, a deficiency in one or more vitamins can result in long-term problems that would require formal treatment. When is a Vitamin Test Prescribed?

Vitamin deficiencies can cause a lot of issues in people. You might consider a vitamin tests online in case you experience any of the following symptoms:

  • Brittle hair and nails- an indication of biotin or B7 deficiency
  • Mouth ulcers or cracks in the corners of the mouth- an indication of deficiency of vitamin B
  • Poor night vision and white growths on the eyes- an indication of vitamin A deficiency
  • Bleeding gums- an indication of vitamin C deficiency
  • Bone loss, tiredness and chronic fatigue, impaired wound healing, bone and back pain- an indication of vitamin D deficiency
  • Pale skin, heart palpitations, and shortness of breath- an indication of vitamin B12 deficiency

Apart from these, a few other symptoms may indicate a vitamin deficiency. They include:

  • Frequent infections and illness
  • Hair loss
  • Scaly patches and dandruff
  • Muscle pain
  • Weight gain
  • Restless leg syndrome
  • Anxiety or depression

All these symptoms are your body’s way of indicating any potential vitamin and mineral deficiencies. Recognizing them with a suitable vitamin test will help you adjust your diet accordingly.

Why is Vitamin Testing Important?

Most people understand the significance of nutrients for overall good health and well-being. However, there are still many who fail to recognize that even severe vitamin deficiencies exist that can lead to serious health issues.

Vitamin D, for instance, is a significant nutrient needed by the body along with calcium and phosphorus to build bones and promote healthy body tissues. Severe vitamin D deficiency may lead to hypocalcemia, causing symptoms such as chronic fatigue and tiredness, weakened muscles and cramps, bone pain, and even depression. Vitamin A is essential for eye health, and its deficiency may result in night blindness. Similarly, vitamin B2 is known to metabolize food and facilitate the development of body cells. A lack of nutrients in the body may cause fissures in the mouth. Low levels of vitamin B6, which helps form red blood cells, may cause anaemia.

So, to avoid these issues, conducting vitamins tests at home is the best and most efficient way to identify which vitamins and nutrients your body lacks. Once you know that, you can make suitable lifestyle changes to increase your vitamin levels and stay fit and healthy.

The exact time required for the test report of your vitamin test to come varies. In some cases, you may receive the report within 48-72 hours. In other cases, the waiting time may be between 5 and 7 days.

As soon as you get the report for your vitamins tests, it would be best if you visit a good physician or nutritionist to get your reports evaluated. An expert will help you identify if there are any vitamin deficiencies in your body. Frequently Asked Questions

Before taking a blood Vitamin Test, you are required to fast for 12 hours. Avoid taking vitamin supplements, and alcohol at least 24 hours before the sample collection.

A vitamin D deficiency is reported when the tested levels of the vitamin are lower than the required range. A level below 10 ng/mL may be termed Vitamin D Deficiency. If your vitamin D levels are low, you must try to get enough of the nutrient through supplements, dairy, meat, sunlight, and fortified foods.

Vitamin B12 is a crucial vitamin that facilitates optimal nerve and brain functioning. It improves hair quality and boosts the development of red blood cells. A Vitamin B12 Test measures the level of the vitamin in the body and identifies the risks due to its deficiency.

Fasting is not required before testing. Patients who have taken B12 injections should avoid testing for vitamin B12 for 2 weeks.

Some studies suggest that a deficiency of vitamin B1 (Thiamine) in the body may reduce appetite. And this may further result in weight loss. However, the information is not conclusive.
